Push-ups, counted by your phone

Put your iPhone on the floor, screen up, under your chest. Lower until your chest is over it, then push. That is a rep, and Reps counted it out loud.

Download on the App Store

No watch, no camera

The proximity sensor already in your iPhone does the counting. It works in the dark, costs nothing in battery, and never asks for a permission.

One set. Two minutes.

Most apps hand you five sets and fifteen minutes. Reps asks for one working set to a number, scaled to you. Want more? Multiple sets is one tap away.

Counts out loud

The screen goes dark at the bottom of every rep because your chest is on top of it. So Reps speaks the count, and your music keeps playing underneath.

A plan that adapts

Built from your measured max. It steps up when you finish and backs off when you do not. Rest days never break your streak.

Everything stays on your iPhone

No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Sessions can be written to Apple Health, and your history exports as a spreadsheet whenever you like.

Free forever

Counting, your adaptive plan, free sets, your streak and 30 days of history are free and always will be. Pro adds the classic programs, full history and more.
