Support

The screen goes black during my set. Is it broken?

No, that is the sensor working. iOS turns the display off while the proximity sensor is covered, and at the bottom of a rep your chest is on top of it. Reps speaks the count out loud so you never need to look. The screen comes back the moment you push up.

My reps are not counting

Check three things. The phone is flat on the floor with the screen facing up. Your chest comes down over the top half of the phone, where the earpiece is. Nothing covers the sensor, so a very thick case or a screen protector that overlaps the earpiece can block it. If it still will not count, choose Tap on the setup screen and tap the screen once per rep.

Where did the counting voice go?

Check the mute button on the session screen, and Settings, then Count out loud. The count plays through the speaker even when the ring switch is silenced, but it ducks under whatever music you have playing.

I want more than one set

One working set is the default because it takes about two minutes. To split your session into sets, tap Multiple sets on the Plan tab or on the screen right before you start. Two to five sets, with rest between them.

Restoring Reps Pro

Open the paywall from Profile, then tap Restore in the top corner. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so a new phone picks them up after a restore. Manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Getting my data out

Settings, then Export as CSV. Every set you have ever done, in a spreadsheet. There is no account and no server, so this export and your iPhone backup are the only copies that exist.

Starting over

Settings, then Erase all data and start over. This cannot be undone, which is why it asks twice.
