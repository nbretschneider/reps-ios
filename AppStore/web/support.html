Support

My reps are not counting

Reps counts with the front camera by default, so it has to be able to see your face. Put the phone flat on the floor, screen up, directly under your face, with the camera end looking straight up at you. You should hear it say "tracking" when it picks you up.

To check the camera on its own, go to Settings, then Counting, then Camera check. It shows how far away your face is in centimetres. If that number moves as you lower and push back up, counting will work. If it says NO FACE, slide the phone until your face is over the camera end.

If you would rather not use the camera, choose Sensor or Tap on the setup screen before you start.

The screen goes black during my set

That only happens in Sensor mode, and it is the sensor working rather than a bug: iOS turns the display off while the proximity sensor is covered, and at the bottom of a rep your chest is on top of it. Reps speaks the count out loud so you never need to look. In the default camera mode the screen stays on the whole time.

Sensor mode does not count on my iPhone

On iPhone 14 Pro and later the proximity sensor sits under the display, and the version apps are given responds far too slowly to catch a push-up. There is no setting that fixes it. Use the default camera mode on those phones, or Tap.

Where did the counting voice go?

Check the mute button on the session screen, and Settings, then Count out loud. The count plays through the speaker even when the ring switch is silenced, but it ducks under whatever music you have playing.

I want more than one set

One working set is the default because it takes about two minutes. To split your session into sets, tap Multiple sets on the Plan tab or on the screen right before you start. Two to five sets, with rest between them.

Is there a paid version?

No. Reps is free in full: every plan, every counting mode, your whole history, the depth charts, widgets, Apple Health and export. There is no subscription, nothing to unlock and nothing to restore on a new phone.

Getting my data out

Settings, then Export as CSV. Every set you have ever done, in a spreadsheet. There is no account and no server, so this export and your iPhone backup are the only copies that exist.

Starting over

Settings, then Erase all data and start over. This cannot be undone, which is why it asks twice.
