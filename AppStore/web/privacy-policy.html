Privacy policy

Effective August 14, 2026

The short version: Reps collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third party code. Everything you do in Reps stays on your iPhone.

What Reps stores, and where

Your workouts, your plan, your records and your settings are stored only on your device. They are included in your normal iPhone backup like any other app data. We have no copy, and no way to get one. Deleting the app deletes everything.

The proximity sensor

Reps counts push-ups with the proximity sensor near the earpiece, the same one that turns the screen off during a phone call. It reports only "something is near" or "nothing is near". Nothing about that signal is recorded beyond the rep counts you see in the app.

The camera in Precision mode

Precision mode is optional and uses the front depth camera to measure how far you lower on each rep. No photo or video is ever captured, stored, displayed or transmitted. The only thing kept is a depth score per rep, on your device.

Apple Health

If you turn on Health sync, Reps writes your sessions to Apple Health as workouts. It never reads your health data. What happens to data in Apple Health is governed by Apple and your Health settings, and you can revoke access at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings.

Export

You can export your history as a spreadsheet from Settings. The export goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

Reps collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Since the app makes no network calls, a change in policy could only ever apply to a future version of the app, never retroactively to data, because there is no data.
